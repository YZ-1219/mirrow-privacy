🪟 Mirrow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

Mirrow ("we", "our", or "us") is a self-reflection and spending awareness app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Mirrow collects only the minimum data needed to provide the app experience:

  • Check-in responses – Your answers to daily reflection questions, stored locally on your device.
  • Journal entries – Personal diary notes you write, stored locally on your device.
  • Spending profile answers – Optional questionnaire answers to improve AI analysis, stored locally on your device.
  • AI analysis requests – When you use the AI Insight feature, your recent check-in data is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a personalized reflection. No personal identifiers are included.

2. Data Storage

All your personal data (check-in records, journal entries, profile answers) is stored locally on your device only. We do not store your personal data on our servers. App images and audio are served from Supabase Storage (public, non-personal assets only).

3. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude API) – Used for AI analysis. Only aggregated, anonymized check-in data is sent. Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy
  • Supabase – Used to serve app images and audio files (no personal data stored). Supabase privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Expo / React Native – Used to build and deliver the app. Expo privacy policy: expo.dev/privacy

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information (no login required).
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use tracking or analytics SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

Mirrow is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
